# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## Product Name
ShinobiLink

## Vision
Build the world's most private and reliable communication platform that works anywhere—even without the internet.

## Problem
Most messaging apps depend on the internet and central servers.

## Solution
A privacy-first, decentralized messaging platform that works over Bluetooth mesh, local networks, and optionally the internet.

## Target Users
- Friends
- Families
- Travelers
- Events
- Emergency situations
- Privacy-conscious users

## MVP Goals
- User profile
- One-to-one chat
- Bluetooth discovery
- Encrypted messaging
- Offline messaging

## Future Goals
- Group chats
- File sharing
- Voice messages
- Voice & video calls
- Wi-Fi Direct
- AI features
